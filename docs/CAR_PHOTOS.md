# Car photos and logos for the home page

The home page drives each car in from the right and out to the left, with its
wheels turning. For that to look right, every photo needs to be prepared the
same way. You only do this once per car.

## The car photo

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Angle** | Exactly side-on (a profile view). Both wheels should look like circles, not ovals. Avoid three-quarter views. |
| **Direction** | Facing **left** is best (the car then drives forwards). A right-facing photo works too: tick "The car in this photo faces right" and the app mirrors it. |
| **Background** | Removed, so it's transparent. On a Mac: right-click the image in Finder → Quick Actions → Remove Background. |
| **Crop** | Tight to the car: the edges of the image touch the front bumper, rear bumper, roof and the bottom of the tyres. No empty space around it. A soft shadow under the car can stay. |
| **Wheels** | Straight (not steered), not blurred, and fully visible (nothing in front of them). |
| **Size** | At least **2000 px wide**; 2400–3000 px is ideal. A 2400 × 700 px car is typical. |
| **File** | PNG or WebP (they keep the transparency). Under 8 MB. |

You don't need to cut the wheels out. After you choose the photo, the form
shows a **Wheels** tool:

1. Drag each circle over a wheel.
2. Drag the black dot on the circle's edge until the circle covers the whole
   tyre (tread included), with the centre on the hub.
3. Press **Spin wheels to check**. The spokes should turn smoothly in place.
   If a wheel wobbles or its edge slides around, nudge that circle until it
   doesn't.
4. Save.

The app cuts those two circles out of the photo and turns them as the car
moves, one full turn for every wheel circumference driven, so they never skid.

## The brand logo

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Background** | Transparent. |
| **Crop** | Tight to the logo, no padding. |
| **Colour** | Any. It's shown very faintly (5% opacity) behind the car, so a solid dark logo works best. |
| **Size** | SVG is ideal. Otherwise a PNG at least **1600 px wide**. |

## How they're sized on screen

You don't size anything yourself: the app does it per screen.

- **Desktop:** the car is about half the screen wide (up to 760 px), with its
  tyres on a ground line 58% of the way down the stage. A tall car (an SUV) is
  scaled down so it never runs into the title.
- **Phone:** the car is 84% of the screen wide.
- **Logo:** 1.13 × the car's width (never wider than the screen), centred
  above the car.

Because every photo is cropped tight to the car, every car sits on the same
ground line and lines up the same way on any device.
